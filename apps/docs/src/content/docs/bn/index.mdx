## The basics

- **Your wallet stays in your control.** You approve every swap in your wallet.
- **Eligible swaps earn points.** A verified swap worth at least $5 that pays the required fee earns 2 WLT Points. These are off-chain points, not transferable tokens.
- **Prize Pool rounds run daily.** Rounds close at 00:00 UTC. Winning rewards become claimable after the distribution is funded and activated.

## Before you start

Read [fees and quotes](/swaps/fees-and-quotes/) and check [what is currently available](/getting-started/product-status/). Walletor is in restricted mainnet testing, and prize funding currently requires the operator's approval.

Swaps carry risk and rewards are not guaranteed. Read the [risk disclosure](/legal/risk-disclosure/).
