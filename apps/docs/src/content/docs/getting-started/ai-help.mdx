title: AI Help and Support
description: Get basic Walletor guidance and contact the support team safely.
---

Open **Need help?** at the bottom-right of Walletor to ask about swaps, wallet connections, WLT points, referrals and Prize Pool claims. On mobile, the chat button sits above the navigation bar.

:::caution[Protect your wallet]
Walletor support will never ask you to send money or share your seed phrase, private key, password or verification codes. Never share these in chat or by email. Legitimate swap, network and claim fees are displayed in the official wallet transaction; support does not ask for a separate transfer to unlock rewards.
:::

## What AI Help can do

The assistant gives basic explanations from reviewed Walletor documentation. It can make mistakes. It cannot access your wallet, check the status of a particular transaction, confirm eligibility, send transactions, issue refunds or credit rewards. Use the official app pages and transaction records to verify amounts and status.

## Contact support

Select **Email support** to open an email to [support@walletor.com](mailto:support@walletor.com). Review and send it in your email app. Chat does not send an email or create a support ticket automatically. If your device has no email app configured, copy the address into your preferred email service.

Include a description of the issue, your device and browser, and a public transaction link if relevant. Do not send secrets. Never repeat a swap solely to resolve missing rewards.

## Chat data

When you send a question, Walletor sends it and up to six recent chat messages to Cloudflare Workers AI to generate an answer. The chat is held in your browser tab's memory and clears when the page reloads; you can also select **Clear chat**. Walletor does not deliberately store chat transcripts or include their content in application logs. Cloudflare processes requests under its service terms. Do not enter personal information or wallet secrets.

Message and request limits apply. If AI Help is unavailable, you can still email support or read the docs.
