The Claim page shows a connected wallet's finalized allocations, active USDC entitlements, round state, and lifetime WLT Points.

## Current status

The mainnet claim service is enabled for Walletor's access-restricted launch. A wallet will show no withdrawable amount until a real round has closed and its official-USDC distribution has been reconciled, funded, staged, and activated.

## Claim flow

When a reviewed distribution is active:

1. Connect the wallet that received the finalized allocation.
2. Review the eligible amount, token, round, and network fee.
3. Request a short-lived claim transaction constructed for that wallet and entitlement.
4. Review and send the transaction in the connected wallet.
5. Wait for confirmed Solana execution.
6. Review the confirmation popup showing the claimed USDC and your lifetime WLT Points, then open the transaction on Solscan if desired.

After confirmation, optional Twitter/X, WhatsApp, and Telegram buttons open a share composer. Sharing is not required to receive winnings and does not happen automatically. WLT Points shown in the popup are off-chain points, not tokens paid by the claim.

On desktop, the primary claim action appears below the Prize Pool display. A submitted transaction can remain pending while verification catches up; submission alone is not confirmed payment. Check the transaction status before trying again. Follow the deadline shown for the specific entitlement—the 24-hour pool round is not the claim deadline.

The transaction can create the wallet's USDC token account when necessary and
then claim the exact committed amount. Walletor validates the returned
signature and on-chain claim account before marking the claim confirmed.

## Fees when claiming

Walletor withholds **2.50% (250 basis points)** from each gross allocation before funding the Rewards Program distribution:

```text
claim fee = floor(gross USDC atomic units × 250 / 10,000)
net claim = gross allocation − claim fee
```

The Claim page displays the net USDC amount. The claimant separately pays the Solana network and any token-account creation cost shown by the wallet. The claim instruction does not send an additional fee from the winner.

The connected claiming wallet is the transaction's fee payer and only required
signer. Keep enough SOL in that wallet for the network fee and, if needed, creation
of its USDC token account. Review the costs in the wallet before approving.

These user-paid costs do not fund deployment of Walletor's randomness program,
oracle requests, or the operator's distribution-funding transactions. Those occur
before a prize becomes claimable and need separate SOL funding.

## Availability

Runtime wallet screening is disabled. Claim preparation still verifies the funded distribution, entitlement, wallet, and claim deadline. An unavailable RPC or an inactive entitlement can prevent the website from preparing a claim; it does not freeze the wallet.

## Why a claim may not appear

An enabled claim page is not an entitlement. Withdrawals require an eligible winning wallet, exact official-USDC reconciliation, verified Rewards Program and round-specific randomness evidence, authority-wallet approval, a funded and activated on-chain distribution, and a confirmed claim window.

## WLT Points are separate

The referral section before the footer shows your connected wallet's permanent invite link, qualified referrals, referral WLT Points, and current lucky-draw weight boost. Open the [Referral page](https://walletor.com/referal) for registration and sharing. A qualified referral awards 100 WLT Points to the referrer; it does not add USDC to a claim. See [Referral rewards](/rewards/referrals/) for the full rules.

WLT Points are not withdrawable winnings. They are off-chain rewards for a future allocation and do not currently represent a transferable asset.

:::danger
Walletor will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Ignore any direct message or transaction claiming winnings are available when the official Claim page does not show a verified entitlement for your connected wallet.
:::
