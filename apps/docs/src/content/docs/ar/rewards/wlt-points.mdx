WLT Points are Walletor's off-chain activity rewards for a possible future allocation. They are not currently a transferable token and do not have a guaranteed monetary value.

## Earning points

Each newly verified, fee-bearing Walletor swap valued at **$5.00 or more** earns **two WLT Points**. Smaller swaps may execute, but do not earn WLT Points or Prize Pool eligibility.

A qualifying credit requires all of the following:

- The swap was requested through Walletor's Jupiter integration.
- Jupiter returned the configured Walletor referral account and fee proof.
- The connected wallet signed the transaction.
- Jupiter returned a successful execution result and transaction signature.
- The confirmed fee is greater than zero.
- Walletor's quote-time USD valuation for the swap is at least $5.00.
- The signature has not already been credited.

Qualified referrals also award **100 WLT Points to the referrer**, once per new referred wallet after its first eligible swap. This is separate from ordinary swap points. See [Referral rewards](/rewards/referrals/) for registration and daily lucky-draw boosts.

## What does not earn points

- Opening or connecting a wallet
- Requesting a quote
- Clicking the Swap button without successful execution
- A failed, rejected, or expired transaction
- A route without the required Walletor fee proof
- A confirmed swap valued below $5.00
- Retrying an already credited transaction signature

## Lifetime and daily values

The Rewards page displays current-round confirmed swaps and lifetime WLT Points from the server ledger. Browser storage is never used as the authoritative points balance.

## Reward tiers

Activity tiers describe progress through confirmed swaps. They do not change the fixed two-point credit per eligible swap. The current Prize Draw ranks two leaders by verified fee contribution and caps other wallets' contribution-based lucky weights at five times the lower median positive contribution. The older tier-multiplier formula is not the current winner-selection rule. See [How we pick the winners](/prize-pool/how-we-pick-winners/).

:::note[No WLT token claim today]
WLT Points are an accounting unit for a future allocation. Walletor has not launched a transferable WLT token, conversion schedule, redemption right, or guaranteed value.
:::
