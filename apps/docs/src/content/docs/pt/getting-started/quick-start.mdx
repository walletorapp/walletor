Walletor is a non-custodial Solana swap interface powered by Jupiter. You keep control of your assets throughout the entire flow.

## 1. Open the official app

Visit [walletor.com](https://walletor.com). Confirm the domain before connecting a wallet or approving a transaction.

## 2. Connect a wallet

Select **Connect wallet**, choose an installed supported Solana wallet, and approve the connection request. A connection lets Walletor read your public address and balances; it does not authorize transfers.

## 3. Choose the tokens

Select the token you want to sell and the token you want to receive. You can search by name or symbol, but a mint address is the safest way to identify a specific asset.

:::caution[Anyone can create a token]
A familiar name or logo does not prove authenticity. Verify the mint address and token status before trading.
:::

## 4. Enter an amount

Enter an amount directly or use the balance controls. Walletor requests a wallet-specific Jupiter order and displays the expected output, price impact, route, fee information, and quote freshness.

## 5. Approve the transaction

When an executable route is available, select **Swap**. Your wallet opens with the final Solana transaction. Review it and sign only if the tokens, amounts, and permissions are what you expect.

## 6. Wait for Jupiter confirmation

Walletor reports a swap as successful only after Jupiter returns a successful execution result with a Solana transaction signature. A button click or wallet prompt is never treated as proof of completion.

## What happens after success

For a verified fee-bearing Walletor swap, the backend records the transaction once, credits its confirmed Prize Pool contribution, adds participant weight, and awards two WLT Points. You can inspect the transaction on Solscan from the success receipt.

Prize Pool eligibility and the two-point credit require a quote-time swap value of at least $5.00. Rounds close every 24 hours from the one-time early close; check the live countdown. The seven-second rewards-sync countdown is an estimate while evidence is checked, not a guarantee of credit within seven seconds.

:::note
The mainnet-USDC Claim page is enabled for Walletor's access-restricted launch. A claim exists only when Walletor activates a reviewed, reconciled, and funded distribution; verified winners then claim from the official Claim page. WLT Points remain off-chain rewards for a future allocation and are not currently transferable or withdrawable.
:::
