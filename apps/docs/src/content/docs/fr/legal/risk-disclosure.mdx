Walletor is software for interacting with decentralized Solana markets. Using it involves technical, market, token, and smart-contract risks.

This disclosure should be read together with the [Walletor legal disclaimer](/legal/disclaimer/).

## Trading risk

Token prices can change rapidly. Slippage, price impact, low liquidity, failed transactions, validator conditions, and third-party routing may produce results different from an earlier quote.

## Token risk

Anyone can create a token or misleading metadata. Verification labels, names, symbols, and logos do not guarantee legitimacy, liquidity, safety, or future value.

## Third-party dependencies

Walletor depends on Solana, Jupiter, Helius, wallet software, RPC infrastructure, and Cloudflare. Their availability, behavior, fees, and security are outside Walletor's complete control.

## Reward risk

Prize Pool eligibility, activity weight, points, or an estimated allocation do not guarantee a payout or financial return. Mainnet-USDC claims are available in restricted testing only for active, funded entitlements. Automatic funding is disabled, and deployed claim-program source verification remains unresolved. WLT Points are off-chain records for a possible future allocation and are not currently transferable, redeemable, or guaranteed to receive value.

## No investment advice

Walletor does not provide investment, tax, or legal advice. You are responsible for assessing trades, local rules, and tax obligations.

## Transaction finality

Blockchain transactions may be irreversible. Review the wallet prompt and all transaction instructions before signing.

## Wallet and key risk

Loss, theft, phishing, malware, compromised browser extensions, malicious approvals, or disclosure of a seed phrase can permanently expose wallet assets. Walletor cannot reset a wallet, restore a seed phrase, or reverse an unauthorized on-chain transaction.

## Regulatory and tax risk

Digital-asset laws, service availability, reporting obligations, and tax treatment vary by jurisdiction and can change. Legal protections may be limited depending on the asset, provider, and location. You are responsible for determining which rules apply to you.

By using Walletor, you accept these risks and remain responsible for your wallet security and trading decisions.
