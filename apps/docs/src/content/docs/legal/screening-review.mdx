## If screening is temporarily unavailable

**Current status — 16 September 2026:** Wallet screening has been removed from swaps, administrator funding, and claims. The screening panel and automatic source refresh are disabled. The guidance below applies to historical screening records and incidents, not current application gates.

An unavailable message means Walletor could not complete the required check or preserve its evidence. It does not mean your wallet was identified as sanctioned. Wait and try again later. Do not repeatedly sign transactions or send funds to an address claiming it can unlock access.

## If an action is restricted

Save the review reference from the message, the public wallet address, the approximate UTC time, and whether you were swapping, funding, or claiming. Preserve any transaction signature if a transaction already occurred. Do not publicly post a complete transaction history or personal documents.

Use Walletor's existing [official community](https://t.me/walletorswap) or [official X account](https://x.com/walletorai) to ask an administrator for the appropriate private support channel. Share only the review reference when requesting that channel. A dedicated compliance intake service and response-time commitment have not yet been established; this page does not promise an immediate review or an automatic release.

Never share a seed phrase or private key. Do not pay an “unlock”, “verification”, or “AML clearance” fee. Confirm the support channel through Walletor's official links; unsolicited direct messages may be scams.

## What review means

An authorized reviewer should compare the reference with the recorded policy and source, confirm which address was screened, and investigate possible errors. A public wallet address or a list association alone does not establish a person's identity. If legal restrictions apply, an internal review cannot override the law.

The current administrator log is read-only and has no button that releases funds, overrides screening, changes a winner, or erases a record. Corrections to screening logic or source interpretation require a documented, reviewed change. The technical controls and support procedures are still being developed for broader launch.

See [AML and sanctions controls](/legal/aml-sanctions/) for the limited scope of current checks and the limits of interface restrictions on existing on-chain claims.
