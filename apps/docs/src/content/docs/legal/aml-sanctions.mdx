Last updated: **16 September 2026**.

Walletor prohibits using its services to facilitate money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions evasion, or the movement of criminal proceeds. This page describes the technical controls currently provided. It is not a statement that Walletor holds a regulatory licence, that every wallet has been identified, or that every transaction has received legal clearance.

## Current screening

Wallet screening is disabled across Walletor, including swap quotes, execution, administrator distribution funding, and claim construction. Missing or expired screening data does not block these actions. AML policy documentation remains available; it does not represent an active screening service or AML clearance.

Before swap submission, Walletor still verifies the selected wallet's transaction signature. This ownership check does not query sanctions data.

Automatic sanctions-source downloads and the administrator screening panel are disabled. Historical source snapshots and screening records are retained. Ordinary wallet signature verification, administrator access controls, claim-program verification, and transaction validation remain active.

## What this does not cover

Walletor currently does not perform wallet sanctions screening, identity verification (KYC), beneficial-owner identification, sanctions ownership/control analysis, indirect exposure tracing, or comprehensive transaction monitoring. The previous screening feature checked only Solana wallet identifiers explicitly listed by OFAC. Historical results do not establish current clearance.

Professional blockchain analytics, jurisdiction-specific requirements, and staffed case-management procedures remain launch work. Where customer identification is legally required, a wallet screening result cannot replace it. Walletor's use of Jupiter does not transfer Walletor's compliance responsibilities to Jupiter.

## Geographic availability and limits

Walletor is unavailable in Serbia. Additional product restrictions may apply, including restrictions on tokenized-stock functionality. A geographic restriction is separate from wallet screening; being able to load a page is not confirmation that a service is lawful in your location.

The current rolling wallet trading limit is a risk control. It is not a legal reporting threshold, KYC exemption, or permission to divide activity across wallets to avoid controls.

## Rewards and existing claims

Walletor no longer screens the funding authority, selected winners, or claiming wallets. Funding and claims continue to require the applicable authorization, verified distribution data, and on-chain transaction checks. Removing screening does not change winners, historical trades, or allocations.

Already funded distributions use a non-revocable claim design. Restricting Walletor's interface does **not** freeze a wallet, reverse a transaction, or guarantee that a direct on-chain claim is prevented. Handling a later designation affecting a funded distribution requires legal and technical review. Walletor does not represent this screening layer as an on-chain sanctions enforcement mechanism.

## Restrictions and review

Historical restriction messages included a review reference. A recorded match is not a public accusation that a user committed a crime. Existing geographic and product restrictions remain separate from the disabled screening feature.

See [Screening restrictions and review](/legal/screening-review/) for the information to preserve and how to request the appropriate support channel. Do not send identity documents, seed phrases, or private keys in community chat.

## Data and accountability

Historical screening records contain the public wallet address, action, request or distribution reference, result, policy version, source fingerprint, and time. No new screening events are generated by swaps, funding, or claims. Existing records and source versions remain retained under administrative access controls; the screening panel has been removed.

See [Screening data notice](/legal/screening-data/) for this feature's data practices and limitations. Routine screening does not automatically file a report with law enforcement.
