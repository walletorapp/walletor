Walletor

What is Walletor?

Walletor is a non-custodial Solana token swap platform using Jupiter routes. Users connect a supported wallet, review a quote and approve each transaction in their own wallet. Eligible swaps earn WLT points and enter daily USDC prize rounds.

Swaps and rewards

A verified eligible swap of $5 or more earns two WLT points. WLT points track reward activity; they are not a guarantee of a cash payout. Prize rounds last 24 hours, with eligible activity recorded by Walletor. MagicBlock supplies verifiable randomness for winner selection under the published rules.

Rewards and winning outcomes are not guaranteed. A round closing does not itself make a prize claimable; prize funding and claim activation must be verified.

How fees fund prizes

Supported swaps currently request a 0.5% total referral fee: 0.4% for Walletor and 0.1% for Jupiter. 100% of Walletor's confirmed swap-fee earnings is allocated back to active users and influencers. Since the October 12, 2026 reserve activation, 89% of Walletor's confirmed swap-fee share funds the Prize Pool and 11% is held in reserve for manual influencer payments, before token-unit rounding. Review the actual quote and network costs before signing.

Availability and security

Walletor is not available in the United States of America (USA). Additional regional restrictions apply. Trading involves risk. Support never asks for your seed phrase, private key or a separate payment to unlock a prize.

For help, email support@walletor.com.
